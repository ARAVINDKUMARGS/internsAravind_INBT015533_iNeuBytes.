About the clinic

A clinic built around continuity of care

Meridian brings together general practice, specialist consultations, and preventive care under one roof, so patients are never passed between disconnected systems.

One record, every visit

Your history follows you between departments — no repeating yourself at every desk.

Same-week access

Most departments offer appointments within 3 working days, not 3 weeks.

Transparent pricing

Consultation fees are shown up front on every doctor's profile — no surprises after.

Medical services

Departments built around common needs, not hospital org charts

Six core departments cover the majority of what brings people in — from routine checkups to ongoing specialist care.

General Medicine

Routine checkups, chronic condition management, and referrals for anything beyond primary care.

Pediatrics

Growth checks, vaccinations, and a waiting room designed to keep kids (and parents) calm.

Cardiology

ECGs, stress testing, and long-term heart health monitoring with the same specialist each visit.

Dermatology

Skin checks, acne and eczema treatment, and mole mapping for early detection.

Orthopedics

Joint pain, sports injuries, and physiotherapy referrals under one care plan.

Dental Care

Cleanings, fillings, and cosmetic consultations in a dedicated dental suite.
