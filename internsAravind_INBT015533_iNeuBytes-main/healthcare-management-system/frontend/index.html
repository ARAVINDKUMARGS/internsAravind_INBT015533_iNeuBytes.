About the clinic

A calmer way to manage your care.

Wellframe Clinic was built to remove the friction from healthcare — no more waiting on hold to book a slot, or hunting for a doctor's availability. Our platform connects patients with the right specialist, gives doctors a clear view of their schedule, and gives administrators the tools to run the clinic with confidence.

Every appointment, record and department is organised in one place — secure, responsive, and built around the people who use it.
